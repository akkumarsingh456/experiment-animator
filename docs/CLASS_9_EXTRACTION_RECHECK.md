# Class 9 Science extraction recheck

## Scope and evidence

Keep the website's existing 12-chapter legacy NCERT Science catalog, as requested.
`CLASS_9_EXTRACTION_RECHECK.json` records a read-only check of accessible uploaded
source pages/chunks: all 12 chapters were located, and all 65 configured topics
had either matched section evidence or a lexical match. No library records,
saved lessons, credentials or source files were changed by the audit.

This is retrieval coverage, not certification of every generated claim. A lexical
match can be sparse. The separately downloaded matching legacy NCERT texts were
also checked with original and flattened whitespace: 64 of 65 topics matched;
Relative density was absent in that source edition, but present in the uploaded
library. Never invent a missing passage to satisfy a coverage count.

## Repairs

- Recover explicit numbered topic headings from flattened page text, including
  display-font spacing and the older "Equations of Motion by Graphical Method"
  title. Keep section boundaries and subordinate explanations.
- Recognise repeated display-font chapter titles at the bottom of opening pages.
  A mention of motion in Force notes must not start the Motion chapter. An
  omitted legacy chapter still terminates the preceding selected chapter.
- Remove confirmed running titles accompanied by an edition and page number,
  including the reported full-year range and spaced SCIENCE/MOTION typography.
- Reject the reported joined summary equations and duplicated graph fractions
  rather than rendering them as trustworthy formulas. Existing originals remain
  available through source review where retained by the shared repair layer.
- For source-attested Class 9 Motion topics, add school-level acceleration and
  constant-acceleration relations with symbols, SI units, conditions and three
  complete derivations. These are curated typesetting/derivation supplements,
  not a guessed reconstruction of an unreadable graph fraction.
- Named motion topics use a straight-line position schematic or a labelled
  velocity–time graph, instead of the broad force-diagram fallback. The graph
  is explicitly illustrative; it contains no fabricated measurement readings.

The relations and scope follow the matching legacy NCERT Motion chapter available
on [IIT Kanpur SATHEE](https://sathee.iitk.ac.in/ncert-books/class-09/nb-science-09/sci-09-chapter-7-motions/),
especially sections 7.3–7.5 (8.3–8.5 in older uploaded editions).

## Activation and checks

Secondary generation cache version is v63. Publish the synced application and
regenerate a lesson to replace its previous cached result. Existing downloaded
PDFs and historical saved lessons are not rewritten automatically.

Focused regression checks cover flattened headings, chapter boundaries, print
furniture, damaged equations, supported formula conditions, diagrams and shared
secondary/senior note/export safeguards. Type checking and production build are
also required. This audit does not exercise every browser workflow or certify
the wording of every future model response.
